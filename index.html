ABOUT US

Creating Extraordinary Water Park Experiences

Welcome to Amaze Products, your trusted partner in engineering high-performance water park attractions and commercial inflatable solutions. Based in Maharashtra, India, we specialize in the custom design, manufacturing, and global import of durable, safety-certified water park inflatables, tubes, rafts, and racer mats.

Built on precision craftsmanship and heavy-duty PVC fabric engineering, our products are engineered to withstand rigorous commercial environments while delivering maximum thrills and safety for operators and visitors alike.

Whether you are outfitting a new aquatic facility or upgrading your existing water slides and splash zones, we combine industry expertise with uncompromising material standards to bring extraordinary water park experiences to life.
